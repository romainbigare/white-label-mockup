# Mockup review — full breakdown of changes

Everything applied from `Mockup_Review_Actions.md`, plus the corrections and
additions that came out of reviewing the result. Every comment id in the action
list is accounted for below, including the handful that were questions rather
than changes.

The companion document [`Mockup_Review_Changes.pdf`](Mockup_Review_Changes.pdf)
shows the same work as before/after screenshots, one block per screen. It is
generated by `node tools/reviewdoc.mjs --before <checkout>` from
`tools/reviewdoc.data.json`; this file is the written record and covers items
that have no screen to photograph.

## 1. The four decisions that carried the rest

Most of the 130-odd comments are consequences of four structural answers rather
than separate edits. Each of these changed a rule, and the rule then decided a
dozen screens.

### 1.1 A task is an advice that has been assigned  ·  `N03` `N04`

Assigning is not a property somebody sets on a task that already exists — it is
the event that brings the task into being.

The build previously took the other reading of WF5.099's "pre-packaged as a
task": a task existed the moment its advice did, and `E1` listed it under
SUGGESTED, unassigned and undated. That put the same item on two screens with
two sets of buttons and made "3 tasks today" a count that included work nobody
had been sent.

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| Assign and Ignore appear on **advice** surfaces only | The D1 card, the advice detail dock. Removed from `E1`'s suggested cards. |
| Mark as complete appears on **task** surfaces only | Removed from the advice card, the advice ⋯ menu and the advice detail dock. |
| A task raised from advice completes through D7 | `E2`'s Mark as done opens the advised-versus-applied record; a hand-typed task opens the plain form. |

`suggestedTasks()` is deleted. `unassignedAdvice()` replaces it, and answers a
different question — which advice has nobody on it — which is what the bulk
assign control acts on.

### 1.2 Coverage is chosen before the survey runs  ·  `C129`–`C134` `C154` `C155`

`A12` stopped asking for a farm name and for what is growing, and now asks the
one question no algorithm can answer: **crops, trees, or both**. It moved from
after the survey to before it.

Asking first is what stops the result coming back full of fallow ground a date
grower then switches off one row at a time. The answer filters the survey
result (`coversKind()` in `survey.js`), `A13`'s pricing, and `F6`'s comparison.

Farms name themselves (`autoFarmName()`) and are renamed in Farm settings.

### 1.3 Structures leave the product  ·  `C116`–`C118`

Not excluded by default — removed. `LAND_USE` is two classes. The survey no
longer generates structure areas, `A11` has no third legend entry, and the
README's description of the survey was rewritten to match.

Reading buildings off satellite imagery is a government-level service, not a
farm one: the farmer is not buying it, cannot act on it, and a villa listed
among his fields is a row he has to think about and then dismiss.

### 1.4 Plans compare by tier, not by column  ·  `S04`

The old `F6` table was two columns, Basic and Pro, with a tick or a cross in
each — and most of its rows read "yes | yes". Sixty rows of two identical ticks
is a feature list wearing a comparison's clothes.

The plan is the row now: each feature group states what Basic includes, then
what **Pro adds**. Every feature prints once. `planCompare` in `content.json`
was rewritten to that shape, and gained an `everyPlan` list for the things that
are simply part of the product.

## 3. Cross-cutting changes

### 3.1 Units and formatting

`area()` returns one unit, hectares carry one decimal, and acres do not exist.
`clock()` and `timeWindow()` are new and respect the F8 time-format setting, so
the irrigation plan, task due times and the activity log all follow it.

### 3.2 Plot naming  ·  `C272`–`C276`

A plot is named after its farm and numbered: **Al Kharj North P1**. The farm
half is looked up at render time in `lPlot()` rather than stored, so renaming a
farm renames its plots — a copy of the name on 32 records is a copy that goes
stale.

- `plot.name` is the whole thing, for lists that cross farms.
- `plot.shortName` is `P1`, for screens already inside one farm.
- The crop is never part of a name (`C279`).
- `advice.plotNames` is derived from `plotIds` rather than stored, for the same
  reason; the fixture's copies were deleted.
- The bidi isolation in `i18n.js` gained an optional hyphen so `P1` does not
  render as `1P` in Arabic.

### 3.3 Session state

`store.js` gained `timeFormat`, `coverage` and `autoAssignTo`.

## 4. Changes made after the first pass

Three came out of reviewing the result rather than from the action list.

### 4.1 No price before a survey  ·  reverses an earlier deviation

The first pass put a starting monthly figure on `A11` for a tree farm, reading
`C144`'s "subscription fee based on area and tree count" as licence to step past
`WF4.091`. **Reverted.** Pricing follows a confirmed scope and never runs beside
one: every Keep, Remove and Join on that screen changes the quantities, so a
figure printed next to them is one the farmer could reasonably be held to and
that would then have to be revised.

### 4.2 Diagnosis and assumptions off every advice screen

`S41` was applied to D2 only in the first pass, on the grounds that the decision
was taken looking at that screen. It now applies to **D2, D3 and D4**: model
inputs with their values are an agronomist's working, and these screens are read
by the person about to open a valve or load a sprayer.

Nothing is lost from the record. Every input is still written to the advisory
log before anyone sees the advice, and the log opens from ⋯ on all three — which
is where `WF5.116` and `WF6.019` are actually satisfied, because an audit trail
the audited party can edit was never one.

That left the assumptions sheet unreachable, and moving it clarified what it is:
efficiency, soil and flow rate are properties of the **plot**. The sheet is now
keyed by plot and opens from Edit on B4's own record.

### 4.3 Two map layer groups removed

**Advanced map layers** (soil type, testing labs) and **Variable rate maps**
(sowing, nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium) are gone in full — the switches,
the session state, the feature keys `gis.advanced` and `vra.maps`, and their
lines in the plan comparison.

A soil polygon is a regional survey at a scale one plot disappears into, and a
laboratory is a place to drive to rather than a layer. A prescription map is a
file loaded into a spreader, not something read on a phone. What is left is what
the map is for: which plots, what they are called, where the trees are, and one
measure painted across them.

## 7. Verification

- `./tools/syntax.sh` — every module parses, every stylesheet balances, and task
  creation has not escaped `advice.js` and `tasks.js`.
- `npm run smoke` — 843 renders across three roles, every overlay opened, every
  farm, plot and tree, five languages, no console errors and no empty renders.
- Spec audits clean: `WF2.002` (no sideways scroll at 360 dp), `WF2.004`
  (48 dp targets), `WF2.006` (16 sp body), `WF2.007` (200% text), `WF2.010` (one
  primary action), and WCAG AA on every rendered string.
- 1,387 strings, translated into all five languages. The new strings were
  machine-produced and are **unreviewed**, like the rest — `WF10.012` still
  wants a named reviewer per language before release.

Two smoke checks were rewritten because they tested behaviour the review
reversed: the A12 assertion now checks the three coverage options rather than
the old crop examples, and the advice-card assertion now fails if any "Mark as
complete" button appears on an advice card at all.
