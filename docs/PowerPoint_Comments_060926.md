# The comments on the v1.5.7 deck — 6 September

Every mark on `Wafra_Farm_App_Screens_v1.5.7_Mark.pptx`, traced back to the thing
it points at. The file carries no PowerPoint comment threads. All of the review
is drawn onto the slides: yellow note boxes, red frames around the element being
discussed, red leader lines, two full-slide strike-throughs, screenshots pasted
in from other apps as references, and — on four pages — screens the reviewer
built himself by cutting pieces out of our own mockups and adding his own text.

The marked file has 68 pages against the clean deck's 58. Pages 3 to 6 are new,
and pages 7 to 12 are a second copy of the guided tour (A4–A4E) that he moved to
the front so he could annotate it; the untouched originals are still at pages 25
to 30.

**74 rows below: 66 changes, 4 marked *Info*, and 4 open questions.** The four
information rows are assumptions and context that ask for no work; they are worth
carrying into the next deck as small boxes beside the screen they belong to, so
the thinking behind a screen travels with it. The four questions need an answer
from Mark before they become anything.

Page numbers are pages in the marked deck.

## Context from the review conversation

Two things came up alongside the deck and shape how several of these read.

The login the reviewer wants is: sign up with an email address; name, phone and
the rest are collected afterwards as additional detail; on returning, Face ID
first, and only if that fails does a code go to the registered **email**. Phone
login goes away. Farm workers are the exception, and they arrive by a different
route — joining from a farmer's invitation code, which is its own run.

And the screenshots pasted onto pages 4, 6 and 15 are taken from other apps. They
are references for the pattern he wants, not artwork to copy.
